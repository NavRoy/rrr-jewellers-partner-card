About Gopal

Partner, and the man behind the counter

Portrait photo

Twenty-two years in the gold trade, most of it spent learning weight, purity and people. I buy the bullion, sit with brides on their designs, and sign off on every hallmark that leaves this showroom.

My grandfather traded gold in Vizianagaram long before there were certificates for it. What he taught me about honest weighing is still the reason customers come back with their daughters.
